Service Level Agreement (SLA) Definition

ASK Project Procedural Engineering Turnaround Standards

🎯 Contractual Baseline: 10 Working Days

SLA stands for Service Level Agreement. In the ASK Field Development Project, the SLA defines the maximum contractual turnaround period allowed for the Client Engineering Team to review and officially return submitted technical & vendor deliverables.

✓ Meet Agreement (≤ 10 WD) Review completed and officially returned with status code (APPR, AWC, etc.) within 10 working days (Mon–Fri, excluding weekends).
⚠ Over Agreement (> 10 WD) Review elapsed time exceeds 10 working days, triggering overdue tracking and potential schedule delay claim liabilities.
How Working Days are Counted:
  • Starts: The first business day following official receipt date (Actual Submit In).
  • Counted: Monday through Friday only. Saturdays and Sundays are excluded.
  • Ends: The date the review status is officially issued out (Actual Submit Out).
